# OEM and redistribution

> **Draft — pending legal review. Not legally approved.** Placeholders in square brackets need lawyer input.

The standard licence covers internal use on infrastructure the licensee owns or controls. These uses need a
separate written OEM or redistribution agreement:

- Embedding koldera-core in a product, appliance or image that is shipped to others.
- Installing it on a customer's infrastructure as part of your product.
- White-label redistribution, resale or sublicensing.
- Offering koldera-core itself as a hosted service to unrelated third parties. [CONFIRM BOUNDARY with "internal
  business purposes" in LICENSE-SCOPE.md]

An OEM agreement sets out, among other things: [PERMITTED DISTRIBUTION CHANNELS], [END-USER LICENCE TERMS THE OEM
MUST PASS ON], [SOURCE CODE ACCESS FOR END USERS — none by default], [BRANDING / ATTRIBUTION], [FEES AND REPORTING],
[THIRD-PARTY LICENCE OBLIGATIONS THE OEM TAKES OVER], [LIABILITY CAP], [GOVERNING LAW].

A redistributor must also pass on the third-party licence texts and notices listed in [NOTICE](../../NOTICE).
