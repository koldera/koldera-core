# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Its text applies in full to every space the project runs: issues, pull requests, discussions and any place where you
represent the project.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, accept feedback, and do not harass anyone.

## Reporting

Write to security@koldera-accessibility.dev (put "Code of conduct" in the subject). Reports are handled privately by the maintainers, and the person who
reports is not named to anyone outside the people handling the report. Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
