koldera-core is available under two licences:

1. **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0** (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). It is free of charge.
2. **A commercial licence** from the owner, for cases the AGPL does not suit.

## Which one do I need?

| You want to... | Licence |
| --- | --- |
| Run koldera-core inside your own organization to check your own documents and pages | AGPL is enough, and costs nothing |
| Study, modify and share it, and publish your changes under the AGPL | AGPL |
| Offer a product or a hosted service (SaaS) built on koldera-core, and publish your own code under the AGPL | AGPL |
| Offer a product or a hosted service built on koldera-core **without** publishing your own code | Commercial licence |
| Embed koldera-core in a product you distribute under terms other than the AGPL | Commercial licence |

The AGPL requires that anyone who lets users interact with a modified or combined version over a network also offers
those users the corresponding source. A commercial licence removes that requirement for your product.

The SDKs and the command-line client (`koldera-cli`, `koldera-js`, `koldera-python`, `koldera-dotnet`, `koldera-go`)
are separate projects under the Apache-2.0 licence. They are planned and not yet published. Using one of them does not
by itself change the licence that applies to the server it talks to.

## How to get one

Write to licensing@koldera-accessibility.dev and describe what you plan to build and how it will be offered. This
page is not legal advice; if you are unsure which licence applies to you, ask us or your own lawyer.
